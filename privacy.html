Privacy

Last updated 9 September 2026

Who we are

MNKY KONNEKT SL, a company registered in Spain (NIF B22561179). Contact: hola@mnkykonnekt.com.

What we use and where it comes from

To prepare a design proposal for a business we use only what that business already publishes: its name, address, opening hours, services and prices, public reviews, and the business email address or phone shown on its website, Google listing or booking page. We do not buy lists and we do not use harvesting software.

Why, and on what basis

We write once to show the proposal, and at most twice more over the following two weeks. In Singapore, business contact information is excluded from the consent rules of the Personal Data Protection Act (section 4(5)), and our volume is far below the thresholds of the Spam Control Act. Under EU law (GDPR article 6.1.f) we rely on our legitimate interest in offering a service to businesses, balanced by the fact that we use public business details only and stop on first request.

How to be removed

Reply remove or unsubscribe to any email. We delete your details and take the draft down the same working day, and we never write again.

What we keep, and for how long

Business details and the draft page: until you ask us to remove them, or 12 months after our last email, whichever comes first. If you become a client: what is needed for the website, invoicing and Spanish tax law, for the time that law requires.

Payments

PayNow, bank transfers and Wise payments are handled by Wise (Wise Asia-Pacific Pte. Ltd.), which holds our SGD account. Card payments, on request, are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number. Every payment comes with an invoice from MNKY KONNEKT SL.

Photos and content

Drafts use stock photos licensed for commercial use and text written by us from public facts. Nothing is copied from your website. Your own photos and text replace them when you become a client.

Analytics

Our pages use privacy-friendly analytics that do not set cookies and do not identify you. There is no advertising tracking.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to our use of it, by writing to hola@mnkykonnekt.com. EU residents can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD).
