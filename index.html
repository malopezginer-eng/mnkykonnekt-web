Questions people ask

How did you get my details?

From your public listing: your website, Google or your booking page. We only use what you already publish. Reply "remove" to any email and we delete your details and the draft the same day.

Where are you?

We are a registered company in Spain and we work Singapore hours. Everything happens by email or WhatsApp, and we usually reply within a day.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name and you get the files. The optional care plan only covers hosting and changes; you can cancel it any time.

Do I need to send photos?

No. Drafts use licensed stock photos. If you have your own, we swap them in; it usually looks better.

Can I have more than one page?

Yes. Most shops need one good page; if you need more (a menu, a booking page, a second location), ask and we quote it.

How do I pay?

By card through a secure payment link, or by bank transfer. You get an invoice from MNKY KONNEKT SL. There is no GST because we are not a Singapore company.

Want a draft for your business?

Send us your shop's name and where it is. If it makes sense, you get a link to a finished draft. No cost, no obligation.
